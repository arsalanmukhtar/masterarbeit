about=Charts, Gemeinde / Kreis / Land comparisons, PDF reports and thematic map layers for official statistics (demography, education, forecasts, living, tourism, employment) of every Gemeinde in Baden-Württemberg. The data ships with the plugin as a GeoPackage, so no database or internet connection is needed (except for the OpenStreetMap basemap). Statistical data: Statistisches Landesamt Baden-Württemberg; boundaries: LGL BW; both under Datenlizenz Deutschland - Namensnennung - Version 2.0. See DATA_LICENSE.md.
